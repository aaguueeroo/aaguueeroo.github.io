Chores

Whose turn is it? Flatto knows.

Add your chores once, with how often they come round and how long they take. Flatto builds the rota and keeps it turning.

  • Fair by time, not by count. A 40-minute kitchen counts for more than a 10-minute bin run.
  • No double shifts. It tries not to give one person two big jobs on the same day.
  • Moves handled. Someone moves in or out and the rota rebalances itself.

Shopping

Stop buying a fifth olive oil.

One list on everyone's phone. Run out of something, add it. At the shop, open the list and tick things off as they go in the basket.

  • Live for everyone. Changes reach every phone straight away, so two people don't buy the same thing.
  • Buy again. Your usual things are one tap away.
  • Who added what. So you know who to ask about "the good sponges".

Before you ask

Fair questions.

Is Flatto out yet?

Not yet. It isn't on the App Store or Google Play. I'm inviting a first group of flats to test it, and you'll get the steps to install it by email.

What does it cost?

Testing is free, and there are no payments in the app.

Does everyone in my flat need it?

Ideally, yes. The rota can only share chores between people who are on it. One person sets up the flat and the others join with a code when they're ready.

What would I have to do as a tester?

Use Flatto in your flat and tell me what works and what doesn't. A short message is plenty.

Will it send me reminders?

Not yet. There are no push notifications right now, so you'll see your turn when you open the app. If you'd want them, tell me. That's what testing is for.

Does it split bills?

No. Flatto shows your rent and bills so everyone sees the same numbers, but it doesn't work out who owes what or move any money.

What happens when someone moves out?

They leave the flat in the app and the rota rebalances between whoever's left. The next flatmate joins with the same code.

What happens to my data?

Flatto has no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and neither does this page. You can delete your account from inside the app. The privacy policy has the details.
